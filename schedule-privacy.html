ROCIs Schedule Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ROCIs Schedule ("the App") — the university timetable, course and assignment planner published by ROCIs Apps ("we", "us") — collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes your information. It covers the Android app (package com.rocisapps.schedule) and the web app at schedule.rocisapps.com.

In short: you can use the App without an account, and then your data never leaves your device. If you sign in, we use your data only to run the App and sync it across your devices. We never sell it, never use it for advertising, and you can delete it at any time from inside the App.

1. Using the App Without an Account

The App opens without asking you to sign in. As a guest, everything you create — courses, class times, events, assignments and semester dates — is stored only in a local database on your device. Nothing is sent to our servers or to anyone else. If you later sign in, that data moves into your account so you don't lose it, and the guest copy is removed from the device.

2. Information We Collect When You Sign In

Signing in is optional and is only needed to back up your schedule and sync it across devices.

Account information

  • Sign in with Google: we request only the basic openid, email and profile scopes. From your Google Account we receive your name, email address, profile picture URL and Google account ID. We do not request access to your Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google data.
  • Email and password: your email address and password. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them.
  • User ID: a unique identifier Firebase assigns to your account, used to keep your data private to you.

Optional profile

  • Name, university and graduation year, if you choose to fill them in.

Content you create

  • Courses: name, code, instructor, credits, colour, semester and (optionally) your grade.
  • Schedule events: title, type (class, lab, exam, study, work, personal), days and times, location, notes and colour.
  • Assignments: title, description, due date, priority and completion status.
  • Semesters: names and start/end dates.
  • Imported calendars: if you paste a calendar (.ics) file, the courses and events in it are stored the same way.

What we do not collect

The App contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and it does not collect your location, contacts, photos, device advertising ID or browsing activity.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To store your schedule, courses and assignments and keep them in sync across your devices.
  • To show class and assignment reminders (scheduled locally on your device).
  • To show your ROCIs Tasks items alongside your schedule, if you also use ROCIs Tasks (see section 5).
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, or training artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

4. Google User Data & Limited Use Disclosure

  • Data accessed: only your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID, received through Google Sign-In.
  • Why: solely to create your account, identify you when you sign in, and show your name and picture in the App.
  • Storage: held in Firebase Authentication and in your private profile record in Cloud Firestore. Google OAuth tokens are managed by Google's sign-in libraries on your device; we do not store them on our servers.
  • Sharing: we do not sell, rent, share or transfer your Google user data to any third party, advertiser, data broker or analytics provider.
  • Deletion: removed when you delete your account (see section 7).
  • Limited Use: ROCIs Schedule's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. How Your Information Is Shared

We share your information only in these cases:

  • Service providers: Google LLC provides Firebase Authentication (sign-in), Cloud Firestore (data storage and sync) and Firebase Hosting (the web app). Google processes this data on our behalf under its terms and does not use it for its own purposes.
  • ROCIs Tasks (our own app): if you sign in to both ROCIs Schedule and ROCIs Tasks with the same account, each app reads your data from the other so you can see your classes next to your tasks — ROCIs Tasks reads your schedule, and ROCIs Schedule reads your open tasks. This happens only for your own account and only while you are signed in.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

6. How We Keep Your Information Safe

  • Your cloud data is stored in Cloud Firestore in the European Union and is protected by security rules that allow only you to read or write it.
  • All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and encrypted at rest by Google Cloud.
  • A copy of your data is cached in a private database on your device so the App works offline.

7. Data Retention & Deleting Your Data

  • Delete your account in the App: go to Settings → Account → Delete account. After you confirm your identity, this immediately and permanently deletes your profile, courses, events, assignments and semesters from our servers, removes your sign-in record, and deletes the copy on that device.
  • Delete by email: you can also ask us to delete your account by emailing support@rocisapps.com from the address on the account. We complete requests within 30 days.
  • Deleting individual items: any course, event or assignment you delete in the App is also deleted from our servers.
  • Retention: we keep your data only while your account exists. Signing out keeps a local copy on that device until you sign in again, delete your account, or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App removes all data stored on the device.

8. App Permissions

  • Notifications: to show class and assignment reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your device.
  • Internet access: to sign in and sync your data when you are signed in.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict its processing. You can edit or delete your data directly in the App, export your timetable as a calendar (.ics) file from Settings, delete your account from Settings, or contact us to exercise any of these rights.

10. Children

The App is intended for university and college students and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Our Website

This website (rocisapps.com) uses Google Analytics to count visits. The ROCIs Schedule app itself does not use Google Analytics or any other analytics service.

12. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page, and for significant changes we will notify you in the App.

13. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, email support@rocisapps.com or visit our Contact Us page.
