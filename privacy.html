Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 26, 2026

Thank you for choosing ROCIs Apps. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, protect and delete your information in our two apps, and how you can control it:

  • ROCIs Tasks — the smart workload and action planner (Android and tasks.rocisapps.com).
  • ROCIs Schedule — the university timetable, course and assignment planner (Android package com.rocisapps.schedule and schedule.rocisapps.com).

Where a section applies to only one app, it says so. We never sell your data or use it for advertising.

1. What Information Do We Collect?

Account information (both apps)

  • Sign in with Google: your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID.
  • Email and password (ROCIs Schedule): your email address. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; we never see or store them.
  • User ID: a unique identifier Firebase assigns to your account, used to keep your data private to you.

Content you create

  • ROCIs Tasks: your tasks and configuration settings, synced and backed up across your devices.
  • ROCIs Schedule: courses (name, code, instructor, credits, colour, semester and optionally your grade), schedule events (title, type, days and times, location, notes), assignments (title, description, due date, priority, completion), semester dates, and an optional profile (name, university, graduation year). Calendars you import from a .ics file are stored the same way.

Using ROCIs Schedule without an account

ROCIs Schedule works without signing in. As a guest, everything you create is stored only on your device and is never sent to our servers. If you later sign in, that data moves into your account and the guest copy is removed from the device.

Information collected automatically (ROCIs Tasks)

  • Device and usage data: device information (such as your mobile device ID, model and manufacturer), operating system, version information and system activity via analytics services.
  • Diagnostics and crash data: crash instances and performance metrics to help us fix bugs and improve stability.

ROCIs Schedule contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs, and neither app collects your location, contacts or photos.

Information from device capabilities

  • Device calendar (ROCIs Tasks): if enabled, we request permission to read and write your device's local calendar. It is used only on your device to create and sync events with ROCIs Tasks; we do not transmit your full calendar to our servers.
  • Notifications (both apps): to show reminders, scheduled locally on your device.

2. How Do We Use Your Information?

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To provide our services: storing your tasks, schedule, courses and assignments and syncing them across your devices, and showing reminders.
  • To connect our apps: showing your ROCIs Tasks items next to your ROCIs Schedule timetable (see section 3).
  • To improve ROCIs Tasks: diagnostic data helps us find crashes and improve performance.
  • To process payments (ROCIs Tasks): RevenueCat handles in-app purchases and subscriptions. We never store your payment card details.

We do not use your information for advertising or profiling, and we never use it to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.

3. Will Your Information Be Shared With Anyone?

We share information only with your consent, to provide our services, to comply with the law, or to protect our users' rights and safety:

  • Google LLC: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Firebase Hosting (both apps); Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (ROCIs Tasks only).
  • RevenueCat (ROCIs Tasks): manages in-app subscriptions and purchases.
  • Between our own apps: if you sign in to both ROCIs Tasks and ROCIs Schedule with the same account, each app reads your data from the other so you can see your classes and tasks together — ROCIs Tasks reads your schedule and ROCIs Schedule reads your open tasks. This happens only for your own account and only while you are signed in.

4. Google API User Data & Limited Use Disclosure

Scopes we request

  • Both apps — basic sign-in (openid, email, profile): to create your account and show your name and picture.
  • ROCIs Tasks — Google Tasks (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks) and Google Calendar (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar), optional: to read, create, update and synchronize your tasks and events between Google and ROCIs Tasks.
  • ROCIs Schedule — Google Calendar, app-created calendars only (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.app.created), optional and requested only when you turn on Google Calendar sync: to create a dedicated "ROCIs Schedule" calendar in your Google account and keep your classes and events in it up to date, so they appear on every device and app connected to your Google Calendar. ROCIs Schedule only changes events it created in that calendar and does not read or change your other calendars.

How we handle Google user data

  • Storage & security: Google OAuth access tokens are kept on your device by Google's sign-in libraries and used only to make requests on your behalf. We do not store your Google password.
  • No third-party sharing: we do not sell, rent, share or transfer your Google user data to any third party, advertising network, data broker or analytics provider.
  • No AI model training: your Google user data is never used to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • Revoking access: you can turn off Google Calendar sync in the app at any time, and revoke our access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Limited Use: the use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs by ROCIs Tasks and ROCIs Schedule will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. How Do We Keep Your Information Safe?

Your cloud data is stored on Google's Firebase infrastructure and protected by security rules that allow only you to read or write it. It is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. ROCIs Schedule's data is stored in the European Union. A copy is cached in private storage on your device so the apps work offline.

6. Data Retention & Your Privacy Rights

Deleting your account

  • ROCIs Tasks: Settings > Privacy & GDPR > Delete My Account & Data.
  • ROCIs Schedule: Settings > Account > Delete account.

Deleting your account permanently removes your authentication record and all your stored data from our servers. You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing support@rocisapps.com from the address on the account. Deleting an individual item in either app also deletes it from our servers.

Retention

We keep your data only while your account exists. After you sign out, a local copy may remain on that device until you sign in again, delete your account or uninstall the app; uninstalling removes all data stored on the device.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data and to object to or restrict its processing. You can edit or delete your data directly in the apps (ROCIs Schedule can also export your timetable as a .ics calendar file), or contact us to exercise any of these rights.

7. Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Our Website

This website (rocisapps.com) uses Google Analytics to count visits.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, email support@rocisapps.com or visit our Contact Us page.
