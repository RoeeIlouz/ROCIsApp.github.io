Designed clean, engineered smart

Offline-First Productivity Tools Built for Focused Work

Students, researchers and makers need tools that clear their heads instead of adding to the noise. ROCIs Apps builds planners that feel quick, calm and reliable, whether you're online or not.

  • Built around action

    Overwhelming to-dos become manageable checklists, with nested subtasks, attachments and flexible repeat rules made for deep focus.

  • Offline first

    Your tools should never stall. Everything saves on your device instantly, syncs in the background, and stays one tap away in Android widgets.

  • Private by principle

    No ads and no selling your data. Your schedules and tasks stay yours, encrypted, and never used to train AI models. See the Privacy Policy.
