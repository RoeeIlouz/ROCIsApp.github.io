How it works

  1. Capture the deadline

    Type it the way you'd say it, or import it from your calendar. Dates, times and reminders are filled in for you.

  2. Break it into steps

    Big assignments become checklists, spread across the days you have left so no single evening gets overloaded.

  3. Work anywhere

    Everything saves on your device first, then syncs in the background when you're back online.

ROCIs Tasks

Where your day's work lives.

Academic deadlines and personal goals, broken into subtasks you can tick off from your phone, your browser or your home screen.

  • One calendar view

    Tasks, Google Calendar and your phone's calendar events together in a single agenda.

  • Plain-language entry

    Type “Submit essay tomorrow at 5pm” and the due date and reminder are set for you.

  • Checklists & attachments

    Split big goals into checklist items and attach notes or images to any task.

  • Home screen widgets

    Tick off tasks and browse your agenda straight from your Android home screen.

  • Works offline

    Changes save instantly on your device and sync in the background when you're connected.

  • Progress you can see

    Track your 7-day completion streak and see how your effort splits across categories.

ROCIs Schedule

Your semester timetable, exams and grades in one place.

Lectures, recurring labs, assignment milestones and exam countdowns, with cloud sync, class reminders and one-tap imports from your university calendar.

  • Weekly timetable

    Colour-coded courses with a swipeable day strip and room details on every lecture.

  • Exam countdowns & GPA

    Pinned countdowns for upcoming exams and a weighted 4.0 GPA that updates as you log grades.

  • .ics import

    Load a whole semester from a Canvas, Moodle or Google Calendar export in one step.

  • Class reminders

    A heads-up before each class with the room number and instructor.

  • Works with ROCIs Tasks

    Assignments and exam prep flow straight into your ROCIs Tasks plan.
