ROCIs Tasks

The central hub for your daily action.

Sync academic deadlines and personal goals into actionable subtasks with cloud-local auto synchronization. Built with a responsive glassmorphic UI, native Android home screen widgets, and interactive calendar agendas.

  • Unified Calendar & Device Sync

    Bring tasks, Google Calendar, and on-device calendar events together into a seamless agenda view.

  • Natural Language Input

    Type "Submit essay tomorrow at 5pm" and let smart parsing automatically configure due dates and reminders.

  • Subtask Checklists & Attachments

    Break large goals down into granular checklist items and attach study notes or images directly to tasks.

  • Interactive Home Screen Widgets

    Check off tasks and navigate calendar agenda views directly from your Android device's home screen.

  • Offline-First Synchronization

    Instant local Hive caching with effortless background Firestore sync when connected.

  • Productivity Analytics & Visual Trends

    Track your 7-day completion streak and balance effort across categories with intuitive charts.
